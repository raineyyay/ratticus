RATTICUS DESKUS

--Run: 
Extract the ZIP, open the folder, open index.html. assets folder must be here or else it won't work.

--Controls: 
A/D or Left/Right = move
hold Space when the human shadow appears
P = pause/resume
If you are on touchscreen, Touch buttons should appear.

--Objective:
Avoid the coffee mug, pens, pencils, and sticky notes.
Candies, paperclips, shields, and clocks are power-ups.
Try to collect cheese bc cheese is yummy!
Hide from the human that sometimes look for their lost items that have already fallen into the void.
Get the highest score.
